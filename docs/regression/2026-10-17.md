main at 1c45581, on macOS, in sandboxes made by `scripts/make-sandbox.sh`. Seven
testers each took a share of the areas. GitHub items read a clone of
pererikbergman/kika; nothing was written to GitHub. No GitLab host was used.

**DOC** the app is right and the item was wrong; the item is now fixed in the
checklist. **SKIP** not run; see "Still to check by hand".
